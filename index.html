✳A tiny sound note: Are “long vowels” actually long?⌄

Some beginner guides use “long vowels” for the extra-stroke ㅑ · ㅕ · ㅛ · ㅠ. They are Y-vowels (ya, yeo, yo, yu), not longer-duration versions of ㅏ · ㅓ · ㅗ · ㅜ. The extra stroke changes the sound, not how long you hold it.
