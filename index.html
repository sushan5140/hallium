About “long vowels”: Some beginner guides use that name for the extra-stroke ㅑ · ㅕ · ㅛ · ㅠ. They are Y-vowels (ya, yeo, yo, yu), not longer-duration versions of ㅏ · ㅓ · ㅗ · ㅜ. The extra stroke changes the sound, not how long you hold it.
