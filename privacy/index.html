HALLIUM / PUBLIC GITHUB PAGES PREVIEW

Privacy Policy.

How this public Hangul Lab and Starter flashcard preview handles local progress, browser speech, hosting requests and external fonts.

Effective date: 22 September 2026 · Applies to the preview at sushan5140.github.io/hallium/ only.

The important part, in plain English

This is a static GitHub Pages demonstration, not the live Hallium account app. Your practice choices and writing sheets are stored in your own browser, not submitted by this preview to a Hallium learning database. GitHub Pages hosts the site, Google serves its fonts, and your browser/device handles speech playback; those providers can process technical request data under their own terms.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy describes only the public Hallium Hangul Lab and flashcard previews under sushan5140.github.io/hallium/, including the original one-book card, the corrected Book V2, and the 12-word Starter Unit 1 deck. It does not describe the separate Hallium account application on Vercel, its Google sign-in, Supabase database, Study Partners, or any other service hosted on a different site. Those features need their own accurate privacy notice before an account-based release.

2. What the preview stores on your device

The preview uses browser localStorage to remember Hangul exploration and practice, saved writing sheets, flashcard reveal and listening steps, self-reported “I know this” or “Learn this” choices, selected words and groups, saved collections, and suggested review dates. In the Starter deck, optional text typed into a sentence field is used to show you your attempt for comparison; this preview does not send it to a Hallium server.

Local storage is associated with this site's origin and this browser profile. It may remain until you reset that preview, clear site data, change browser profiles, or the browser removes it. Another device or browser will not automatically have the same progress. Other sites on the same GitHub Pages origin may technically share that browser-storage origin; don't use these previews to store confidential information.

The Starter deck stores progress separately from the Book V1, Book V2, and Hangul Lab demos. “Reset deck” clears only that deck's own record. Browser settings can clear the entire site's records if you prefer.

3. Hosting, fonts and technical requests

GitHub Pages serves the site files. A request to load a page or asset typically exposes technical information to the host, such as IP address, requested URL, time and browser information, under GitHub's practices. Read GitHub's privacy statement ↗.

Google Fonts supplies the preview's DM Sans and Noto Sans KR web fonts, so a browser may contact Google when displaying the pages. Read Google Fonts privacy FAQ ↗. We do not control these providers' logs, cookies, retention or network handling.

These static previews contain no Hallium-operated analytics tracker, targeted advertising script, or remote study-progress submission in the code reviewed for this release. This does not mean the external hosting and font services receive no technical data.

4. Korean pronunciation audio

When you press a listening button, the preview passes the Korean word or example sentence to your browser's speech-synthesis interface. Depending on your browser, device, chosen voice and settings, speech processing may happen on-device or through a speech service operated by the browser or operating-system provider. We do not receive a voice recording from this feature, and this preview does not ask for microphone permission. If your device has no suitable Korean voice, playback may not work or may sound inaccurate.

5. Why information is used and shared

Local learning records are used only to make your own preview interactive: to restore your selected card, show saved words, present review dates and display your learning choices. The preview does not have an account or a way to send these records to a study partner. Requests to the host, font provider or speech service are handled by those providers as described above.

We do not ask for your name, email, password or payment information in the GitHub Pages flashcard preview. Please don't enter real personal information into optional sentence fields or writing-sheet names.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Use each preview's reset control to clear its own learning record, where provided.
  • Clear the site's local data using your browser's site-data/settings controls to remove all Hallium GitHub Pages preview records in that browser.
  • Choose not to press Listen if you don't want to use your browser/device's speech service. You can also block external fonts in your browser, although typography may change.
  • To ask about this preview or request a correction, use the Hallium GitHub Issues page ↗. Issues are public: do not post passwords, health records or other sensitive data there.

Clearing your browser data cannot erase technical request logs that GitHub, Google, or a speech provider may independently retain. Consult the relevant provider's privacy information for those requests.

7. Children and sensitive information

This is a general educational preview and does not deliberately ask children—or anyone else—for personal details. Learners who require adult guidance under applicable rules should use it with a parent or guardian. Do not upload or enter sensitive personal information; there is no reason to do so to learn the vocabulary.

8. Updates and contact

We may revise this notice when the preview's functionality, providers or data handling change. We will update the effective date above and publish the revised text at this URL. Adding sign-in, cloud sync, community sharing, or AI processing would require a specific notice before those features are included in this preview.
