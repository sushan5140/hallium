# Hallim community contribution and editorial review

## What this release does
Hallim Community provides a public pathway and a *local-only* proposal composer.
Copying a proposal does not submit it to Hallim, establish review status or publish it.
There is no database, automated inbox or open comments queue for community submissions in H-P15.

## Editorial gates for a future reviewed intake
1. **Rights** — accept only original material or material the author is licensed to submit; retain source attribution.
2. **Privacy** — reject identifying learner records, private conversations, photos or recordings of other people without consent.
3. **Learning quality** — check Korean grammar, meaning, register, dialect nuance and learner level before any publication.
4. **Evidence** — do not convert community opinions into verified scores, official TOPIK readiness, or individual mastery.
5. **Moderation** — no hate, harassment, stalking, sexualization of minors, targeted insults, private information, spam or impersonation.
6. **Review separation** — creators, ambassadors and learners may suggest content but cannot publish to shared curriculum directly.
7. **Transparency** — approved edits need an internal editor decision, provenance and a reversible versioned change.
8. **Abuse prevention** — any later server intake must use authentication as appropriate, rate limits, moderation, idempotency and RLS before launch.

## Consent and creator content
No monetization promise, follower ranking, automatic attribution to an individual or public display of a learner's profile is implied.
Private pilot feedback stays in the existing invited creator-feedback route and must not be surfaced on a public community page.

## Release scope
This is a contribution *preparation* workflow and claim-safe educational hub, not a social network or production submission pipeline.
